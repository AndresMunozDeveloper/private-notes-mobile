# 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Private Notes (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) handles information when you use our mobile application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This app is designed to function without collecting, storing, or sharing any personal data.

# 2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal information, including but not limited to:

Name
Email address
Phone number
Location data
Contacts
Photos or media
Device identifiers (such as advertising IDs or IMEI)
Usage analytics
Crash reports linked to users
The App operates entirely on-device and does not require user accounts or sign-in.

# 3. Internet and Network Usage

This App does not use internet connectivity and operates fully offline.

# 4. Third-Party Services

This App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

# 6. Data Storage

Since no data is collected, no personal data is stored on external servers.

Any preferences or settings saved by the App are stored locally on the user's device and are not accessible to us.

# 6. Children's Privacy

This App does not collect any data from users of any age, including children under the age of 13 (or applicable local age thresholds). Because no data is collected, parental consent is not required.

# 7. Security

Since all functionality occurs locally on the device and no data is transmitted, user information remains under the user's control.

# 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in an updated version within the App or its store listing.
